FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account? +

No account needed. Dozr works completely offline — your data stays on your iPhone and never leaves it.

Does it work for prescription medications? +

For anything you need to take regularly: prescriptions, vitamins, supplements, or herbal remedies. Dozr is not a medical app — it's a habit and organization tool.

How many supplements can I add for free? +

The free plan allows up to 5 items — enough for most routines. For more complex setups, Pro offers unlimited items.

Is my health data private? +

100%. Nothing leaves your device. No cloud, no account, no company can access your history. Your health habits are yours alone.

How do I cancel Pro? +

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Dozr Pro. Cancel anytime, no hassle. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.
