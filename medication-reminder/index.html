iPhone · iOS 16.6+

Use the schedule you were given

Enter your existing medication routine and reminder times. The app is an organization tool and does not determine treatment, dosage or changes to your schedule.

Allow notifications during setup

Check that notifications are enabled for Dozr in iPhone Settings. Review your Focus settings if alerts are not appearing as expected.

Record after taking an item

Mark an item in the Today screen after taking it. A notification is a prompt; the entry is your own record. The app cannot verify that a medication was taken.

Keep the scope clear

Dozr stores your routine on your device and does not require an account. It is not a tool for diagnosis, medication interactions or advice about a missed dose. Start free with up to five items.
